✦ Who We Are

A clean space, a clearer day.

Araujo's Cleaning Services LLC was created to provide customers with dependable, detail-oriented cleaning. Led by Ana Carina Santos Castañeda and Antonio José Araújo Barrios, the company is built around the belief that a clean space contributes to comfort, organization, productivity, and peace of mind.

Our approach is centered on treating every property with care and adapting service to each customer's specific needs — from everyday cleaning to more detailed cleaning projects, with consistent results every time.

✦Our Commitment

Reliable, professional cleaning with attention to detail, consistency, and respect for every property we serve.
